═══ PHASE 1 — SCOPE & PLAN ═══

### 1. Restated Research Question
Provide a **comprehensive, multi-dimensional dataset** on Tesla, Inc., covering its financial performance, operational metrics, technological advancements, market position, regulatory challenges, and strategic initiatives. The dataset must include **historical trends, current status (2023–2024), and forward-looking projections**, supported by primary and secondary sources.

---

### 2. Research Type
**Exploratory + Factual + Technical**
- **Exploratory**: Uncover non-obvious insights (e.g., supply chain vulnerabilities, R&D trade-offs).
- **Factual**: Compile verifiable data (e.g., revenue, production volumes, regulatory filings).
- **Technical**: Analyze engineering, software, and manufacturing innovations (e.g., 4680 battery, Full Self-Driving).

---

### 3. Sub-Questions (6)
1. **Financial Performance & Valuation**
   - What are Tesla’s **revenue, net income, gross margin, and free cash flow** for 2019–2024?
   - How does Tesla’s **market capitalization and P/E ratio** compare to legacy automakers (e.g., Toyota, Volkswagen) and EV peers (e.g., BYD, Rivian)?
   - What are the **primary revenue streams** (automotive sales, energy, services, regulatory credits), and how have they evolved?

2. **Operational Metrics & Production**
   - What are Tesla’s **annual vehicle production and delivery numbers** by model (Model 3/Y, S/X, Cybertruck) and region (US, China, Europe, Other) for 2019–2024?
   - What are the **key production challenges** (e.g., Gigafactory ramp-ups, supply chain bottlenecks) and their impact on output?
   - How do Tesla’s **manufacturing costs per vehicle** compare to industry averages, and what are the drivers of cost efficiency?

3. **Technological Innovations & R&D**
   - What are the **specifications, advantages, and limitations** of Tesla’s core technologies (e.g., 4680 battery cells, structural battery packs, Full Self-Driving (FSD) v12)?
   - How does Tesla’s **R&D spending** compare to peers, and what are the focus areas (e.g., AI, robotics, energy storage)?
   - What are the **timelines and milestones** for upcoming products (e.g., Robotaxi, Optimus humanoid robot, next-gen vehicle platforms)?

4. **Market Position & Competitive Landscape**
   - What is Tesla’s **global EV market share** by region and vehicle segment (2019–2024)?
   - How do Tesla’s **brand perception, customer satisfaction, and loyalty metrics** (e.g., Net Promoter Score, J.D. Power rankings) compare to competitors?
   - What are the **key competitive threats** (e.g., BYD’s price advantage, legacy automakers’ EV transitions, Chinese EV startups)?

5. **Regulatory & Legal Challenges**
   - What are the **major regulatory risks** facing Tesla (e.g., US EPA/NHSTA investigations, EU CO₂ targets, China data localization laws)?
   - How have **autopilot/FSD-related accidents** impacted Tesla’s legal standing and insurance costs?
   - What are the **trade and tariff risks** (e.g., US-China tariffs, EU anti-subsidy investigations)?

6. **Strategic Initiatives & Future Outlook**
   - What are Tesla’s **key strategic priorities** for 2024–2026 (e.g., AI integration, energy business expansion, Robotaxi network)?
   - How does Tesla’s **energy storage business** (Megapack, Powerwall) contribute to revenue and growth?
   - What are the **biggest existential risks** to Tesla’s long-term dominance (e.g., technological disruption, geopolitical fragmentation, talent retention)?

#### **Layer 3: Source Depth**
- **Primary Sources**: Tesla 10-K/10-Q filings, earnings call transcripts, NHTSA/EPA reports, patent filings.
- **Secondary Sources**: Bloomberg, Reuters, The Verge, CleanTechnica, Seeking Alpha, J.D. Power reports.
- **Tertiary Sources**: Used only for anecdotal insights (e.g., Reddit discussions on FSD beta testing).

#### **Layer 4: Cross-Source Synthesis**
- **Agreements**: E.g., all sources confirm Tesla’s 2023 revenue growth slowed to ~19% YoY (vs. 51% in 2022).
- **Conflicts**: E.g., Tesla claims FSD v12 is "Level 2+" autonomy; critics argue it lacks true Level 4 capabilities.
- **Gaps**: E.g., Limited public data on Tesla’s in-house AI chip (Dojo) performance metrics.
