## Quick Commands

| Say | Does |
|-----|------|
| `[item] [amount]` | Records an expense |
| `income [amount] [source]` | Records income |
| `saldo` | Shows all account balances |
| `net worth` | Shows total wealth |
| `monthly summary` | Income vs expenses this month |
| `add account [name] [balance]` | Registers a new account |
