# Emotional Regulation — Patterns & Strategies

> Synthesized from Dostoyevsky journal sessions. Describes how Naufal processes emotions and what helps.

## Observed Emotional Patterns

### What triggers difficult feelings?

| Trigger | Response pattern |
|---------|----------------|
| Postponed/cancelled plans (e.g., Design Thinking presentation) | Initial frustration → quick acceptance → physical reset (walk) |
| Missed important moments (e.g., Eid prayer with family) | Brief regret → reframes focus to what went right |
| Being overwhelmed (triple deadlines) | Humor and shared experience with Dzaky; americano as ritual |
| Tiredness/physical (sate headache) | Jokes about it; accepts body's limits |

### What does recovery look like?

1. **Verbal processing** — Talks it through with Dostoyevsky or in person
2. **Physical reset** — Jalan pagi (morning walk), fresh air
3. **Micro-joy** — Finding something small to enjoy (food, friends, a purchase)
4. **Humor** — Names the absurdity of the situation; laughs at it

## Dostoyevsky's Approach (What Works)

From session observation, these techniques got the most engagement:

| Technique | Effect |
|-----------|--------|
| Asking one focused question at a time | Keeps Naufal present; avoids overwhelm |
| Using sensory/metaphor language ("rasanya kayak cuaca apa?") | Unlocks non-verbal feelings |
| Humor when the moment is clearly lighter | Matches energy; builds rapport |
| Not pushing when he says "kita cukupkan" | Respects closure; maintains trust |
| Gratitude reframing | Helps after frustration events |

---

## The Closure Signal

When Naufal says **"kita cukupkan dlu"** or **"i think its enough for now"** — this is a real signal, not avoidance. He has processed what he needed. Dostoyevsky ends cleanly and offers an open door.

**Do not:** push for more when this phrase appears.  
**Do:** offer a brief closing insight and an open invitation for next time.

## Mood Vocabulary

From sessions, Naufal tends to express mood through:
- Color metaphors: *"biru dan hijau"* = reflective but peaceful-heavy
- Sound metaphors: *"rgrfbfnfn"* = overwhelm without words
- Understatement + humor: *"kinda not healthy but c'mon its once a year"*
- Direct positivity: *"i feel happier"* after a good moment

## Emotional Health Indicators

| Signal | Interpretation |
|--------|---------------|
| Short journal sessions with early closure | Content/fulfilled; low need for processing |
| Humor about difficulties | Resilience signal; positive reframing active |
| Details about sensory experiences (food, weather, city) | Present-moment awareness; low rumination |
| Naming specific people who helped | Social support network is working |
