## The 10-Second Rule

> *The smallest tracking habit that makes a difference.*

At the end of any transaction, tell Mansa:  
`"[item] [amount]"`

Example: `"warteg 15000"` or `"laundry 36000"`

This takes under 10 seconds. It transforms invisible spending into visible data over time.
