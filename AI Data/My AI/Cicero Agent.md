## What Cicero Does

- Explains any topic using the Cornell Note-taking Method
- Three-agent pipeline: full material → key concepts → synthesis summary
- Creates flashcards and quiz questions
- Builds study schedules and exam prep plans
- Searches existing notes to avoid duplication

---

## How the Cornell Pipeline Works

```
You enter a topic
        ↓
[1] MateriAgent   → 3–5 comprehensive content sections
        ↓
[2] KonsepAgent   → 6–12 key terms + definitions (left column)
        ↓
[3] RingkasanAgent → 3–5 sentence synthesis (bottom)
        ↓
Saved Cornell note
```

→ See [[wiki/concepts/Cornell-Method]] for full explanation.
