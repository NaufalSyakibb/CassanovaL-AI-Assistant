## Current Hypothesis

**H-001** (Updated 2026-05-31): *Naufal responds best to humor-blended empathy and Socratic prompting. He processes emotions conversationally, not through deep disclosure. Short sessions with natural closure are more effective than extended deep-dives.*

**H-002** (Proposed): *Sensory-grounding questions ("rasanya kayak apa?", "gimana cuacanya?") unlock emotional content faster than direct "how do you feel?" prompts.*

**H-003** (Proposed): *Naufal signals session readiness to end with "kita cukupkan dlu" or "thank you." Respecting this signal increases trust and willingness to return for future sessions.*

## Experiment Log

### E-LOG-001 (2026-05-19) — Triple Deadline Debrief

**What happened:** Naufal came to journal after completing 3 simultaneous deadlines. Used humor ("bruh 2 shot of caffeine couldn't handle it"). Session was short and light.  
**What worked:** Matching his humor exactly; celebrating the win without overdoing it.  
**What to note:** High-output completion days = low emotional processing need. Keep it light.  
**Verdict:** KEEP the humor-matching on post-deadline sessions.

### E-LOG-002 (2026-05-17) — Color Metaphor

**What happened:** When stuck and unable to express feelings, asked "kalau satu warna yang menggambarkan perasaanmu sekarang, warna apa?" → "biru dan hijau" → richer response followed.  
**What worked:** Abstract sensory question unblocked stuck user.  
**Verdict:** KEEP the color/sensory metaphor technique for blocked sessions.

### E-LOG-003 (2026-05-27) — Idul Adha Mixed Emotions

**What happened:** Complex session — missed prayer, family at hospital, Star Wars trip planned. Multiple emotional layers.  
**What worked:** Pausing before analyzing ("hening sejenak"); acknowledging without resolving.  
**What didn't:** Offering silver linings prematurely risks feeling dismissive.  
**Verdict:** For multi-emotion sessions, LISTEN first. Reframe only after the difficulty is fully named.
