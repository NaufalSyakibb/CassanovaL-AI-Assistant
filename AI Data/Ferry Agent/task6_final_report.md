## Ringkasan Eksekutif
The relationship between Friedrich Nietzsche’s philosophy and Nazi Germany is a complex and contested topic in intellectual history. While Nazi ideologues selectively appropriated and reinterpreted Nietzsche’s ideas—particularly the *Übermensch* and the *will to power*—to justify their racial and nationalist agenda, these interpretations often diverged from Nietzsche’s original intent. Scholars remain divided on the extent of Nietzsche’s influence on Nazism, with some arguing that his philosophy was fundamentally misrepresented, while others contend that its ambiguity enabled its exploitation. This article examines Nietzsche’s core ideas, their selective adoption by Nazi Germany, and the ongoing debates surrounding this fraught legacy.
